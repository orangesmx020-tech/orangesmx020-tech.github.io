About Me

I’m an M1 student in the Komuro Laboratory at Chiba University, working on Wi-Fi CSI sensing.

I’m interested in detecting whether someone is present, recognizing activity, indoor localization, and how CSI sensing performs in different environments and on different devices.

Outside research, I like PC hardware, gaming, music, and technical projects I can tinker with myself. Feel free to get in touch about research, tech, or a game together.

ESP32-S3 Wi-Fi CSI Sensing Platform

An ESP32-S3-based Wi-Fi CSI experimental platform. It currently supports real-time CSI acquisition, experiment and device management, UDP data collection, historical storage, raw CSI inspection, and I/Q, amplitude, and phase visualization.

Next step

The platform will be used for upcoming presence detection, activity sensing, and cross-environment experiments.
