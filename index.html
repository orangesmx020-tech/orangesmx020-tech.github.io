01 / About

A little about me

My current work focuses on building practical sensing systems that can understand human presence, activity, and environmental context without relying on wearable devices.

I am particularly interested in robust sensing systems that can generalize across environments, devices, users, and time.

Beyond research

Outside research, I enjoy gaming, computer hardware, and building technical projects that are fun to tinker with. If you want to talk about wireless sensing — or just play some games — feel free to reach out.
